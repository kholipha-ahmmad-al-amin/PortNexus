    Frequently Asked Questions on Trade Compliance & L/C Auditing

    Direct answers to high-intent questions regarding export documentary compliance, UCP 600 rules, and customs clearance.

    What does PortNexus do?

    PortNexus compares selected fields extracted from trade documents and flags missing or differing values for qualified review. It does not decide bank acceptance, guarantee payment, or determine customs clearance.

    How does PortNexus audit documents under ICC UCP 600 Article 18(c)?

    PortNexus can compare extracted invoice and credit descriptions. A difference is routed for qualified review because automated text comparison alone cannot decide whether the descriptions correspond under the credit and applicable banking practice.

    How does the candidate HS code feature work?

    The current sample classifier returns limited keyword candidates. Bundled duty rates are unverified demonstration data and must not be used for a customs declaration.

    How can autonomous AI assistants connect to PortNexus via Model Context Protocol (MCP)?

    PortNexus exposes a stateless Model Context Protocol endpoint at https://portnexus.equisaas-bd.com/api/mcp using Streamable HTTP JSON-RPC. Protected operations require an organization API key.

    What is the PortNexus Pre-Customs Clearance Inspection Certificate?

    When evidence persistence is enabled, PortNexus can produce a PDF with a QR link and HMAC-SHA-256 seal that authenticates a stored audit snapshot. It does not certify customs clearance or guarantee acceptance by a bank.
